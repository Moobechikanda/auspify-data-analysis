LIMITATIONS
-------------------------
This dataset describes catalogue titles. It does not provide viewership, subscriber demographics, revenue, licensing costs, or profitability. Therefore, the analysis cannot establish which titles are most watched or most profitable.
Country and genre counts overlap because a title can have multiple country and genre associations.
Release-year counts measure the years titles were released, not the dates they were added to Netflix or produced.
Findings describe this dataset and should not automatically be treated as a complete representation of Netflix's current catalogue.
